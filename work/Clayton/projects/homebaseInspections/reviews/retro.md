# Clayton Homebase Inspections Reviews - Retro

## Abstract
Three things that went right and three things that went wrong.

Good:
- The application for all intents and purposes is better than when from where we started, the code base after merge,
despite adding all of the home inspections code actually came in 500 lines smaller than it was.

- The relationship that we have with Servicing feels good, and strong despite all of the negatives.

- The project (Homebase Inspections) seems to have stirred a lot of interest and people who are not currently stake
holders seem interested in the applicaiton

Bad:
- Assumptions all the way down, at the begining of this project we knew that we were making a lot of assumptions, and
not only that we were making a lot of assumptions, but we knew that there was a good chance that they would be wrong.
I think one of the fundemental assumptions that just getting inspectors inspections would be the first and easiest step
was a bad assumption that we based all of the rest of the work around.

- Time to itterate. We believed, wrongly that we could itterate quicker than what we could on the I-Series, when it was
clear that what was initially handed to us was untested and frankly not working, we tried to address the issues.
But the time it took us to make changes ranged between 'a week' to 'weeks'.
